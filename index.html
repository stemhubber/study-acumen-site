About us

Built in South Africa, for South African classrooms.

— The Study Acumen team

Study Acumen grew out of a STEM tutoring programme. We kept seeing capable learners who couldn't get help at 9pm the night before a test, and tutors spending their evenings marking instead of teaching.

So we built one place for both. Learners get patient, step-by-step help aligned to CAPS and to the way NSC papers are actually marked. Tutors get classes, lessons, tasks, papers and a full marking workspace, with AI doing the first pass so they can spend their time where it matters.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Which grades and subjects do you cover?

Grades 10, 11 and 12 Mathematics and Physical Sciences, aligned to the South African CAPS curriculum.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every account gets a free daily AI allowance plus up to two free top-ups a day. You only pay if you want more AI than that.

Do I need a tutor to use it?

No. The AI tutor, Practice Arena, Cramming Tutor, Test My Theory, Explore It and Career Checkpoint all work on your own. Joining a tutor's class adds their lessons, videos and marked tasks.

Is the AI's mark my final mark?

No. AI marking gives you fast feedback to learn from. When it's a class task, your tutor reviews it and has the final say.

Can my learners keep sending work on WhatsApp?

Yes. Export the WhatsApp chat and import it into Study Acumen. The photos are matched to the right learners' submissions, ready to mark.

Does AI marking decide the final mark?

No. The AI gives a first-pass mark and comments on the script. You review and adjust every one before releasing, and you can send a script to a colleague for moderation.

Who pays for AI marking?

When you turn on auto-marking for a task, it uses your AI allowance, not your learners'. Most tutors start on the free daily allowance and top up only in busy weeks.

How do I pay for credits?

Choose an amount on the Credits page in the app and pay by EFT using the reference shown. Once we've verified the payment, the credits appear in your account. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Study Acumen runs in your browser on phone, tablet or computer, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.
