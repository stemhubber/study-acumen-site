How do I find the turning point of y = x² − 6x + 5?
Let's do it step by step.
1. The axis of symmetry is x = −b ÷ 2a = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.
2. Substitute: y = 9 − 18 + 5 = −4.

About us

Built in South Africa, for South African classrooms.

Study Acumen grew out of a STEM tutoring programme. We saw the same thing again and again: learners who were capable of much more but couldn't get help at 9pm the night before a test, and tutors spending their evenings marking instead of teaching.

So we built one place for both. Learners get patient, step-by-step help aligned to the CAPS curriculum and the way NSC papers are actually marked. Tutors get classes, lessons, tasks and papers in one workspace, with AI doing the first pass on marking so they can spend their time where it matters.

  • Improvement over marks. We celebrate progress — 42% to 48% counts.
  • Understanding over answers. The tutor explains; it doesn't just hand over the solution.
  • Teachers stay in charge. AI marking is a study aid and a first pass — the tutor has the final say.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Which grades and subjects do you cover?

Grades 10, 11 and 12 Mathematics and Physical Sciences, aligned to the South African CAPS curriculum.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every account gets a free daily AI allowance plus up to two free top-ups a day. You only pay if you want more AI help than that.

How do I pay for credits?

Choose an amount on the Credits page in the app and pay by EFT using the reference shown. Once we've verified the payment, the credits appear in your account. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

Can AI marking replace my teacher?

No — and it isn't meant to. AI marking gives a fast first pass and feedback to learn from. Your tutor or teacher reviews it and has the final say.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Study Acumen runs in your browser on phone, tablet or computer, and can be added to your home screen like an app.
